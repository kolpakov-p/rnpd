== Scheduling pods on RunPod ==

To deploy a pod to RunPod, your Deployment/Pod MUST have BOTH:

1. nodeSelector — to target the virtual node:

   spec:
     nodeSelector:
       kubernetes.io/hostname: virtual-runpod

2. toleration — to bypass the NoSchedule taint:

   spec:
     tolerations:
       - key: "virtual-kubelet.io/provider"
         operator: "Equal"
         value: "runpod"
         effect: "NoSchedule"

Without the toleration, the pod will be rejected by:
  - Kyverno ClusterPolicy (if installed) — adds anti-affinity to prevent scheduling
  - The kubelet's isSystemPod() check — skips pods without this toleration

Full example:

  apiVersion: apps/v1
  kind: Deployment
  metadata:
    name: my-gpu-workload
  spec:
    template:
      spec:
        nodeSelector:
          kubernetes.io/hostname: virtual-runpod
        tolerations:
          - key: "virtual-kubelet.io/provider"
            operator: "Equal"
            value: "runpod"
            effect: "NoSchedule"
        containers:
          - name: worker
            image: my-image:latest
            resources:
              limits:
                nvidia.com/gpu: "1"
